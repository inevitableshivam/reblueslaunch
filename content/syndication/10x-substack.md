# Three launches, one story: what changed for 10x

10x had a clear product and a crowded category. Many AI app builders promised some version of “turn a prompt into an app.” The useful question for its launch was: what would someone remember after scrolling past the film?

We framed 10x as “Shopify for apps.” The first launch led with founder Evan Yadegari’s story. The second showed the opportunity to build an app business. The third introduced 10x Web and a new reason to care: building, testing and publishing from any device.

Reblues made three founder-led launch films across four months. Motion graphics carried the product explanation, while each opening line gave the audience a reason to keep watching.

The three X launch posts recorded **791K+ combined public views** in our October 2026 case-study snapshot, along with 2,464 likes, 1,413 bookmarks, 373 replies and 112 reposts. These are post metrics, not a claim about attributed sales or a promise of future reach.

The practical lesson: a product can launch repeatedly without starting its story from zero. Keep the category clear and change the proof as the product develops.

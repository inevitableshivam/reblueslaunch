How a launch runs

From first idea. To launch day.

  1. 01 The launch film

    Ideation & scripting

    We start with your launch goal and buyer, then shape the one idea the film needs to land.

    • Product, ICP, objections and the launch moment
    • Creative concepts and opening hooks
    • Script and storyboard approved before production

  2. 02 The launch film

    Production & editing

    Our San Francisco crew films founder-led footage when the brief calls for it. We handle production through the final edit.

    • The shoot, design, motion and sound
    • Edit and color in your brand language
    • A master film and cutdowns for each feed

  3. 03 Launch + Amplify

    Creators & influencers

    For Launch + Amplify, we match creators to your goals and ICP, brief them with shareable assets, and coordinate the first wave.

    • A shortlist built for your audience
    • Where your package includes list review, you can veto a creator and we'll replace them
    • Reach targets agreed upfront and measured after launch

  4. 04 The launch film

    Launch day

    We plan back from the date and run a timed release across your channels. Your team knows what happens in the first hour.

    • Distinct cuts and post copy for X and LinkedIn
    • A first-hour run of show for your team
    • With Amplify: creator wave, live adjustments and a report
